## Shot description

A model in a navy satin abaya, matching navy chiffon hijab and black kitten heels walks
left to right along a cobblestone street past a red-brick wall and a dark wooden door.
The camera tracks with her and ends on a close-up of the satin sheen and the wide sleeve cuff.

- Palette in frame: deep navy (≈ `#1f2140`), brick red (≈ `#9a5a48`), warm stone grey (≈ `#8c8780`), black.
- Mood: quiet, elegant, everyday luxury; soft overcast light.
- Natural beats for text or motion: 0–1.5 s full-length walk; 1.5–3.5 s step down off the kerb; 3.5–4.9 s fabric and sleeve close-up.
